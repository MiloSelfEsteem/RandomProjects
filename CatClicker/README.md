CatClicker by Milo/MiloSelfEsteem/45g
A lightweight, high-performance AutoClicker and basic Macro tool.



HOTKEYS

These keys work globally (even when the app is minimized):

    Toggle AutoClicker: F6 (Default).

    Toggle Recording: F7 (Default) - Requires "Enable Recording" to be checked.

    Toggle Playback: F8 (Default) - Requires "Enable Recording" to be checked.



HOW TO USE

    Basic Auto Clicking: Set your interval, choose your click type, and press the Start hotkey.

Macro Recording:

        Check "Enable Recording?"

        Press the Record hotkey, perform your actions, and press it again to stop.

        Press Playback to repeat your actions. Use "Loop Playback" for infinite looping.

Advanced: Use "Anti-Detection" to add a 20% random jitter to your click speed to help bypass basic anti-cheat systems.



IMPORTANT NOTE ON MODES

To prevent system conflicts, CatClicker runs one mode at a time:

    You cannot Record or Playback while the AutoClicker is running.

    You cannot start the AutoClicker while a Macro is playing back or being recorded.

    You cannot Playback while recording.

    If a hotkey isn't responding, ensure the other modes are stopped.
